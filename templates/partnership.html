FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Have a different question? Include it in your application and we'll discuss it with you.

Start a conversation
Who can apply to partner with Ken Dala?

Professional tour operators, destination management companies and providers of distinctive travel experiences are welcome to introduce their services.

Do you accept partners outside your current destinations?

Yes. Our current destination focus is Kazakhstan, Turkey and Oman, but we are interested in qualified partners in other markets.

Who delivers the actual tour?

The local operator delivers the services agreed for each tour. Ken Dala manages customer-facing communication and booking coordination.

How are commissions and payments handled?

Commercial terms, payment timelines and settlement arrangements are discussed and agreed with each partner before tours are published.

How are cancellations or changes managed?

We coordinate customer requests with the operator. Specific cancellation, refund and notification responsibilities are established in the partnership agreement.

What happens after I submit an application?

We review the information provided and, if there appears to be a good fit, contact you to discuss the next steps. Submission does not guarantee acceptance.
