HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good to know.

Find quick answers to common questions before getting in touch.

Can I contact Ken Dala outside business hours?

Yes. You can send a WhatsApp message or contact form request at any time. Our team responds during published human support hours.

What if I need help during my tour?

Use the local operator contact supplied in your booking confirmation for immediate on-trip operational issues. You can also contact Ken Dala for escalation and follow-up. Emergency availability depends on the arrangements confirmed for your booking.

Can you arrange a customized itinerary?

Yes, you can describe your preferences in the form. We'll review the request and discuss possible options with our destination partners.

Where can I find my booking reference?

Check your booking confirmation email or reservation documents. If you cannot find it, include the email address used when booking.
